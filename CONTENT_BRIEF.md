# CONTENT_BRIEF.md

## Purpose

This file contains the first version of the website copy.

Main language: French.  
Tone: direct, professional, commercial, credible.  
Goal: convert visitors into freelance leads.

The website must sound like a serious Cloud / DevOps freelance consultant, not like a CV.

## Global Positioning

Badis Merakchi is a Cloud, DevOps and Platform Engineering consultant based near Geneva.

Core positioning:

> Consultant Cloud & DevOps freelance, basé près de Genève, j’aide les entreprises à automatiser, fiabiliser et industrialiser leurs infrastructures cloud et leurs workflows de livraison.

Short version:

> Consultant Cloud & DevOps freelance à Genève et en remote.

Commercial promise:

> Des infrastructures plus fiables, des déploiements plus propres, des opérations mieux automatisées.

## Accuracy Rules

Do not claim:

- Certified Kubernetes expert
- Senior Kubernetes specialist
- Security expert
- Official partner of any company
- Guaranteed cost reduction
- Guaranteed response from prospects
- Client names without approval
- Deep expertise not supported by experience

Acceptable claims:

- Cloud and DevOps consultant
- 4 years at Oracle Geneva
- Experience with Terraform, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes exposure
- Swiss client context
- Financial sector exposure
- Practical delivery mindset

## Final Copy Direction

The final website copy should be:

- Short
- Sharp
- Business-oriented
- Technically credible
- Easy to scan
- Stronger than a CV
- Less verbose than this context file
